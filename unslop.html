Designed to stay on your wrist

Four practical choices we made after testing ordinary crystal bracelets and sleep trackers.

No charging cradle

The disc draws a tiny pulse from your phone only during the half-second you tap it, so there is no battery to degrade and no Bluetooth radio on your skin.

No monthly membership

Your daily natal reading and tarot pull stay included for the life of the bracelet. We never lock your reading behind a $7 monthly paywall.

Lobster clasp instead of elastic

Stretch cords fray and snap after a few months. We string the amethyst side on steel jewelry wire and finish the chain side with a solid lobster clasp.

Easy to gift without birth time

Each bracelet ships unlinked in its linen pouch and drawer box. Your recipient enters their own birth date on their first tap.
